How Your Care Works

Simple, clear steps from first visit to recovery

1. Assessment

Detailed history, movement check, and discussion of your goals so treatment targets the real cause of your problem.

2. Personal Plan

A customized program with exercises, hands-on therapy, and advice that fits your schedule and lifestyle in Bahawalpur.

3. Progress & Support

Regular review of your progress, adjustments when needed, and guidance so you can manage symptoms confidently.
