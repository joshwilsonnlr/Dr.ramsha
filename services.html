Back & Neck Pain Physiotherapy

Evidence-based assessment and exercise therapy for back stiffness, neck pain, and posture-related strain.

What causes it

Extended sitting, poor postural habits, lifting strain, muscle tension, and joint stiffness commonly contribute to spinal pain.

How we can help

A thorough assessment identifies what is driving your pain. Treatment includes hands-on therapy, targeted exercises, and postural education.

Assessment process

We evaluate your history, symptoms, movement patterns, and strength to create a personalized rehabilitation plan.

When to seek help

Consult a professional if pain radiates down an arm or leg, causes numbness or weakness, follows an injury, or persists beyond a few days.

Joint Pain & Rehabilitation

Specialized care for knee, shoulder, hip, and other joint pain, stiffness, and movement limitations.

What causes it

Joint pain can result from overuse, previous injury, prolonged stiffness, or natural changes in joint cartilage over time.

How we can help

Treatment focuses on reducing pain, improving range of motion, and restoring strength through exercise and hands-on therapy.

Assessment process

We evaluate your history, symptoms, movement patterns, and strength to create a personalized rehabilitation plan.

When to seek help

Seek professional evaluation if a joint is swollen, hot, locked, unstable, or if pain disrupts sleep.

Graded rehabilitation for sprains, strains, and overuse injuries with safe return to activity and sport.

Sudden twists, overtraining, weak stabilizing muscles, and returning to activity too quickly are common injury causes.

How we can help

Evidence-based rehabilitation restores strength, proprioception, and confidence. We work toward a safe, graded return to your sport.

Assessment process

We evaluate your history, symptoms, movement patterns, and strength to create a personalized rehabilitation plan.

When to seek help

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Guided recovery after orthopedic surgery, following surgeon's protocol and working with your medical team.

What causes it

After surgery, tissues heal, but joints can become stiff and muscles may weaken. Everyday movements often feel challenging initially.

How we can help

We provide structured, progressive rehabilitation that follows surgical guidelines and your surgeon's recommendations.

Assessment process

We evaluate your history, symptoms, movement patterns, and strength to create a personalized rehabilitation plan.

When to seek help

Contact your surgeon immediately for fever, increasing swelling, wound drainage, or severe pain.
