Back & Neck Pain Physiotherapy in Bahawalpur

Assessment and exercise-based care for back stiffness, neck pain and posture-related strain.

Common causes

Long hours of sitting, poor posture, lifting strain, muscle tightness or joint stiffness can all contribute to spinal pain.

How physiotherapy may help

Physiotherapy looks at how your spine, muscles and daily habits are working together, then uses manual techniques, mobility work and strengthening to help reduce pain and improve movement.

Assessment

Your history, symptoms, movement and strength are assessed so the plan fits you.

When to seek evaluation

See a professional if pain spreads into an arm or leg, comes with numbness or weakness, follows a fall or accident, or is not settling after a few days.

Joint Pain Physiotherapy in Bahawalpur

Care for knee, shoulder, hip and other joint pain, stiffness and reduced movement.

Common causes

Joint pain can follow overuse, injury, long-standing stiffness or changes in joint health over time.

How physiotherapy may help

Treatment aims to restore comfortable movement and strengthen the muscles that support the joint, with exercises you can continue at home.

Assessment

Your history, symptoms, movement and strength are assessed so the plan fits you.

When to seek evaluation

Seek evaluation if a joint is swollen, hot, locked, giving way, or if pain is waking you at night.

Sports Injury Physiotherapy in Bahawalpur

Rehabilitation for sprains, strains and overuse injuries, with a graded return to activity.

Common causes

Sudden twists, overtraining, weak supporting muscles or returning to sport too quickly are common causes of injury.

How physiotherapy may help

Rehabilitation progresses from protecting the injury, to restoring movement, to rebuilding strength and control for your sport or activity.

Assessment

Your history, symptoms, movement and strength are assessed so the plan fits you.

When to seek evaluation

Get assessed if you cannot put weight on a limb, notice significant swelling or bruising, or the same injury keeps returning.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation in Bahawalpur

Guided rehabilitation after orthopaedic and related surgery, working with your surgeon's advice.

Common causes

After surgery, joints and muscles can become stiff and weak, and everyday movements can feel difficult.

How physiotherapy may help

A structured programme gradually restores range of movement, strength and confidence, paced to your surgeon's guidance and your healing.

Assessment

Your history, symptoms, movement and strength are assessed so the plan fits you.

When to seek evaluation

Contact your surgeon or doctor urgently for fever, increasing swelling, wound problems or sudden severe pain.

Neurological Rehabilitation in Bahawalpur

Movement-focused rehabilitation to support balance, strength, coordination and daily function.

Common causes

Conditions affecting the nervous system can change strength, balance, coordination and the ease of everyday tasks.

How physiotherapy may help

Therapy focuses on practising meaningful movements, improving safety and independence, and teaching patients and families useful exercises.

Assessment

Your history, symptoms, movement and strength are assessed so the plan fits you.

When to seek evaluation

Sudden weakness, facial droop, speech difficulty or severe headache needs emergency medical care, not physiotherapy.

Muscle & Soft Tissue Injury Physiotherapy in Bahawalpur

Treatment for muscle strains, ligament sprains, tendon irritation and persistent soft tissue pain.

Common causes

Strains and tendon irritation often come from sudden effort, repetitive tasks or muscles that are tired or underprepared.

How physiotherapy may help

Care combines hands-on techniques, activity guidance and progressive loading to help tissues recover and cope with demand.

Assessment

Your history, symptoms, movement and strength are assessed so the plan fits you.
