What to expect at your visit

1. Conversation & history

Discussion of symptoms, how they started, and your goals.

2. Movement assessment

Observation of posture, range, strength, and functional movements.

3. Clear plan

Explanation of findings in simple language and a treatment plan.

4. Treatment & home advice

Hands-on care and/or exercises, plus guidance for between visits.

When should I book physiotherapy?

Consider booking if pain or stiffness limits work, sleep, or daily movement; if recovery after injury or surgery feels slow; or if posture-related discomfort keeps returning.
