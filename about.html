About Dr. Ramsha

Dr. Ramsha Ramzan is a physiotherapist who helps patients in Bahawalpur recover from injury, manage pain and move more comfortably.

She believes every patient is different. Her treatment plans are built around individual goals, lifestyle and clinical findings, and she takes time to explain what is happening so that progress continues beyond the appointment.
