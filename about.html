How She Treats

Every patient starts with a proper assessment. Treatment may include manual therapy, therapeutic exercise, posture correction, mobility work, and clear home guidance.

The goal is practical: less pain, better movement, and a plan you can understand and follow.

Who She Helps

Patients with back or neck pain, joint problems, sports injuries, post-surgical stiffness or weakness, neurological movement challenges, and posture-related discomfort — including students, office workers, athletes, and older adults in Bahawalpur.

Why patients choose evening care

Many people in Bahawalpur cannot leave work or studies during the day. Evening clinics from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM (Monday–Saturday) make it practical to get assessed and start treatment without missing regular responsibilities.

Dr. Ramsha’s approach is personal: listen carefully, examine movement, explain findings in simple language, and build a plan you can follow at home between sessions.
