Meet Dr. Ramsha Ramzan

Dr. Ramsha Ramzan is a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) and NeuroMusculoSkeletal specialist providing physiotherapy and rehabilitation services in Bahawalpur.

She currently practices at Moeen Medicare and Infertility Hospital, Satellite Town, Bahawalpur. Her clinical experience includes work at hospitals and rehabilitation settings across Bahawalpur and Bahawalnagar, including Agile Institute of Rehabilitation Sciences, Bahawal Victoria Hospital, Iqbal Hospital, Arif Hospital, DHQ Hospital Bahawalnagar, and Life Care Medical Complex.

Her focus is restoring movement, reducing pain, and helping patients return to work, sport, and daily life with confidence.
